01 / About

Engineering decisions under uncertainty.

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Structural Engineering at the University of Alberta, specializing in risk and reliability analysis, numerical modelling, and pipeline integrity. My research combines probabilistic methods, Bayesian decision analysis, reinforcement learning, and value-of-information analysis to improve inspection and maintenance planning for pipelines affected by stress corrosion cracking and fatigue.

I enjoy translating rigorous reliability models into practical decision-support tools that can be used by researchers and integrity-management professionals.
